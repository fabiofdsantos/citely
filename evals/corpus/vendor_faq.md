## General questions

Vendors frequently ask whether a self-assessment is enough for systems that are
not high-risk. Systems outside the high-risk classification are not subject to
the conformity assessment procedure.

Vendors also ask about voluntary codes of conduct. Providers of systems that are
not high-risk are encouraged to apply the high-risk requirements voluntarily.
